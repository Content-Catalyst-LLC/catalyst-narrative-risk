# Migration from v1.6.0

The v1.7.0 migrator accepts a schema-valid v1.6.0 canonical record and rebuilds it under the v1.7.0 method and schemas.

The analytical result, evidence ledger, narrative map, and human decision are preserved. No stakeholder actors, relationships, incentives, pressures, conflicts, or consequences are fabricated from the former scalar `stakeholder_pressure` field. Historical stakeholder intelligence must be added explicitly within a persistent case.

The migrated record remains exactly reproducible and records v1.6.0 provenance in its migration metadata.
