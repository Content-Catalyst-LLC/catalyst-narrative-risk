# Narrative Risk Review Checklist

Before using a claim in publication, reporting, strategy, or decision support, review:

1. Claim wording: Is the claim narrow enough for the evidence?
2. Source base: Are there enough sources, and are they appropriate?
3. Evidence strength: Does the evidence support the level of confidence implied?
4. Uncertainty: Are assumptions and unknowns visible?
5. Freshness: Is the claim time-sensitive or fast-changing?
6. Pressure: Are incentives or reputational concerns shaping the interpretation?
7. Consequences: What happens if the claim is wrong?
8. Review: Who reviewed it, when, and with what decision?
