# Migration from v1.9.0

v1.10.0 accepts schema-valid v1.9.0 canonical records and reproduces their analytical score, risk level, evidence ledger, narrative map, method snapshot, and human decision exactly under the v1.10.0 contract.

Hardening records are not fabricated during migration. Privacy policies, retention assessments, backup manifests, accessibility reports, performance reports, and production-readiness reports must be created from current operator evidence after the upgrade.
