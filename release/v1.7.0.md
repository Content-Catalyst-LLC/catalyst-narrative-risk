# Catalyst Narrative Risk v1.7.0

## Stakeholder, Incentive, and Pressure Intelligence

This release adds an inspectable actor and relationship layer to persistent Narrative Risk cases.

### Added

- Structured stakeholder actors with interests, influence, stance, disclosure, and external identifiers.
- Typed stakeholder relationships and dependency graphs.
- Evidence-linked incentive, conflict, pressure, and consequence records.
- Advisory actor-pressure ranking, flags, and suggested stakeholder-pressure level.
- Catalyst Canvas stakeholder handoff with preflight reference validation.
- Persistent SQLite tables, REST endpoints, CLI commands, WordPress controls, and portable-bundle support.
- Deterministic migration from v1.6.0 without inventing historical stakeholder records.

### Preserved boundaries

The v1.6.0 scoring, evidence-ledger, narrative-map, governance, and monitoring policies are unchanged apart from their version identifiers. Stakeholder intelligence does not infer motives, certify claims, alter scores automatically, or approve publication.
