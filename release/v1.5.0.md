# Catalyst Narrative Risk v1.5.0

## Review, Approval, and Governance Workflow

This release adds institutional review governance around persistent cases and immutable analytical revisions.

### Added

- Versioned review templates and canonical stage ordering.
- Reviewer assignments, due dates, acceptance, completion, waiver, overdue state, and reviewer queues.
- Role-based permissions embedded in the method snapshot.
- Append-only stage and final decisions.
- Conditional approval, required wording, disclosures, and publication restrictions.
- Approval validity, expiration, reassessment flags, and publication eligibility.
- Governance REST endpoints and command-line operations.
- Browser-local WordPress governance workflow for demonstration and portable bundle exchange.
- Governance workflow, assignment, decision, and template JSON Schemas.
- Deterministic v1.4.0 analytical-record migration.

### Preserved

The v1.4.0 scoring weights, thresholds, evidence-ledger policy, narrative-map policy, analytical outputs, and six-layer record model are unchanged. Governance records are separate from the risk score and cannot be inferred from it.
